Q3 Infrastructure Review

Where the platform spend went, what it bought, and the two decisions that need making before the next planning cycle.

18 MINUTES
FIGURES AS BILLED
THE NUMBER
$1.4M

Annualised run rate on compute after the migration, against a $1.9M forecast. The gap is almost entirely reserved-instance coverage that landed a quarter early.

SOURCE: FINOPS LEDGER, SEPTEMBER 2026
WHERE IT WENT

Three cost centres moved; the rest held flat

Compute fell 26% after the reserved-instance purchase, but the saving is one-off and does not repeat next year without a further commitment.

Storage grew 41%, driven almost entirely by retention on the events pipeline rather than by user growth.

Egress is now the second-largest line and the only one still compounding month over month.

Source: FinOps ledger and vendor invoices, Q1–Q3 2026
DECISION ONE

Retention on the events pipeline

Ninety-day retention costs roughly $18K a month and is read in under two percent of investigations. Thirty days would cover every incident reviewed this year.

Against that, compliance has asked for a written position before any reduction, which has not been drafted.
