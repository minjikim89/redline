Internal · Platform Engineering

Q3 Infrastructure Review

Where the platform spend went this quarter, what it bought, and the two decisions that need making before the next planning cycle opens.

18 MINUTES
FIGURES AS BILLED, NOT AS FORECAST
The headline number
$1.4M

Annualised run rate on compute after the migration, against a $1.9M forecast. Almost the entire gap is reserved-instance coverage that landed a quarter earlier than planned, which means it is a timing win rather than a structural one.

Source: FinOps ledger, September 2026 close
Where it went

Three cost centres moved; everything else held flat

Compute fell 26% after the reserved-instance purchase. The saving does not repeat next year without a further commitment, so treating it as a new baseline would overstate next year's headroom.

Storage grew 41%, driven almost entirely by retention settings on the events pipeline rather than by user growth. Per-user storage actually fell over the same period.

Egress is now the second-largest line and the only one still compounding month over month. Nothing in the current roadmap slows it.

Source: FinOps ledger and vendor invoices, Q1 through Q3 2026
Decision one

Retention on the events pipeline

Ninety-day retention costs roughly $18K a month. Fewer than two percent of investigations read anything older than thirty days, and every incident reviewed this year was resolved inside that window.

Against that, compliance has asked for a written position before any reduction. That position has not been drafted, and drafting it is the actual blocker rather than the engineering work.

Source: incident review log and storage telemetry, 2026 year to date
Decision two

Whether to renew the managed search contract

The contract renews in November at a 22% uplift. Self-hosting the same workload models at about 60% of the renewal price, but adds an on-call surface the team has said it cannot absorb this half.

A third option nobody has costed: drop the two least-used indexes, which together account for a third of the bill and serve one internal dashboard.

Source: vendor quote dated August 2026; internal capacity model
What we are asking for

Two decisions, one owner each

Retention: a compliance position by mid-October, so the change can land before the December peak rather than after it.

Search: a decision by the end of October, which is the last point at which self-hosting could be stood up before the contract renews.

Neither decision needs new budget. Both need someone named against them.
